📋 Purpose of the app

Driver's Log records trips by car, by bike or on foot for private or business purposes – manually or by GPS recording. Trips can be reviewed and exported as PDF or CSV.

The app requires no user account and stores no data in a cloud. The developer receives none of your trip, address or location data.

💾 Data stored locally

All data is stored only locally in a database on your device. This includes:

  • Trips (date, driver, purpose, start, destination, distance, note)
  • Address book (label, street, town, country, coordinates)
  • Drivers (name) and trip purposes (label)
  • GPS recordings (waypoints with time, position, altitude, speed and accuracy)
  • Sync log (which entries were changed on which of your devices)
  • Settings, including a personal OpenRouteService API key if you add one

This data only leaves the device through the functions described below, which you use or set up yourself.

✅ No cloud storage  ·  ✅ No account

🗂️ Backup and export

Automatic backup

If enabled, the app saves a daily copy of its database to a folder (Android: chosen by you via the system folder picker; Windows: “Documents\Fahrtenbuch\Backups”). The backup stays there even after uninstalling and can be restored in the app. If this folder is synced with a cloud service (e.g. Google Drive or OneDrive), that service's terms apply.

Export to PDF or CSV

Export files are saved on your device. Whether and with whom you share them is up to you; the app you choose is used for that.

🌐 External services (network access)

The app only contacts the following services when you use the corresponding function. For technical reasons your device's IP address is also transmitted to the service. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR (providing the function you requested).

Nominatim – OpenStreetMap Foundation

When you create or edit an address, the address text you enter is sent to Nominatim to obtain suggestions and coordinates. If the search finds nothing, the app (Android only) may determine your current position once and send it to Nominatim to identify the country of the address. The app does not store this position.
Privacy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

OSRM – route calculation

To calculate a distance, the coordinates of start and destination are sent to the public server router.project-osrm.org. No account is needed.
Project: project-osrm.org

OpenRouteService (optional)

If you have entered your own API key in the settings, the coordinates of start and destination are sent together with that key to OpenRouteService (HeiGIT gGmbH, Heidelberg, Germany) instead of OSRM.
Privacy: openrouteservice.org/privacy-policy

Start and end of a GPS recording are matched against your address book on the device; no data is transmitted for this. There is no automatic background retrieval.

📶 Wi-Fi sync (Pro)

Optionally, the app syncs your data directly between your own devices on the same Wi-Fi network (e.g. Android phone and Windows PC) – without the internet and without third-party servers.

  • To find other devices, the app regularly broadcasts a short message on the local network containing a random device ID and the device name (Android: device model, Windows: PC name).
  • Trips, addresses, drivers and purposes are transferred between the devices; during initial setup the entire database may be transferred.
  • Transfer on the local network is not encrypted. Only use sync on trusted networks (e.g. your home network).
  • So that syncing also works with the screen off, Android runs a background service with a visible notification. If you agree, the app asks to be excluded from battery optimisation.
  • On Windows, the installer adds a firewall rule for private networks and the local subnet only.

🛒 In-app purchase (Pro version)

The Android app offers an optional upgrade to Pro through a one-time in-app purchase (product ID: driverslog_pro). Payment is handled exclusively by Google Play. The developer receives no payment data, only the order information Google provides for the purchase (e.g. order number and product).
Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

✅ Payment data only with Google  ·  ✅ No subscription

⭐ In-app rating (Android)

After a number of saved trips or a successful export, the app may show Google Play's rating dialog once. The dialog is provided entirely by Google; the app does not learn whether or how you rated it. A submitted review is public, as usual on the Play Store.

🔒 Android permissions

  • INTERNET – address search (Nominatim) and route calculation (OSRM, optionally OpenRouteService)
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – checks whether a Wi-Fi or internet connection is available
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE / NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES – finding devices for Wi-Fi sync on the local network
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION – GPS recording of trips and tours, and the one-time country lookup during address search (see above)
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC – GPS recording and Wi-Fi sync continue while the app is minimised, each with a visible notification
  • WAKE_LOCK / REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS – prevents Android from stopping the sync in idle mode; you grant the battery exception yourself
  • com.android.vending.BILLING – in-app purchase via Google Play
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – added by the Qt framework and only relevant on Android 9 and 10; the app stores its data in its own app storage

The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts or photos. Location is only accessed during a GPS recording and in the single case described for address search.
